What is Konpa?

Caribbean Roots,
Nairobi Spirit

Partner Work & Connection
Live DJ Sessions

Konpa (also Compas or Kompa) is a modern méringue rhythm born in Haiti in the 1950s. It weaves together African polyrhythm, Cuban son, and French Caribbean musical tradition into one of the world's most sensual partner dances.

At KCL Studio, we bring that tradition to Nairobi — authentic teaching, real music, and a community that celebrates movement. Whether you're stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or refining advanced footwork, our classes meet you exactly where you are.

Partner & Solo Styles
Learn both lead and follow. Our curriculum covers partner work, solo styling, footwork, and musicality training.
Real Music, Always
KCL's in-house DJ plays authentic Konpa and Caribbean tracks every single session — you learn to dance to the real sound.
Global Technique, Local Community
Internationally recognised Konpa technique delivered inside Nairobi's most vibrant social dance community.
